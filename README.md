Project Overview :

This project analyzes food delivery sales and customer data to understand ordering patterns, customer behavior, and business performance. The analysis helps identify popular food items, peak order times, customer preferences, and revenue trends.

Objectives :
Analyze food delivery sales data.
Identify top-selling food items.
Understand customer ordering behavior.
Discover peak order times and locations.
Generate business insights using data analytics.

Tools Used :
Python
Pandas
Matplotlib
Jupyter Notebook

Key Insights "
Most ordered food items were identified.
Customer spending patterns were analyzed.
Peak ordering hours and busy days were detected.
Sales trends and revenue performance were evaluated.

Conclusion :

The project provides valuable insights into food delivery sales and customer behavior, helping businesses improve customer satisfaction and increase sales performance.
